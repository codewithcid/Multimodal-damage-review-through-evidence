FROM PHOTO TO EVIDENCE

How it works

The image is the source of truth. A claim provides context; user history adds review flags without changing what the photo shows.

  1. 01

    Submit the evidence

    Choose car, laptop, or package; add up to three photos and describe the damage. The server checks image readability and prepares the photos and claim for Gemini.

  2. 02

    Inspect with vision

    Gemini reviews the submitted photos and claim against the required evidence for that object. Structured output records the visible damage, affected part, severity, and supporting image IDs.

  3. 03

    Apply the guardrails

    Deterministic checks validate the response, handle unusable images, flag instruction-like text, and add claimant-history risk context. History alone cannot establish visible damage.

  4. 04

    Review the verdict

    See supported, contradicted, or not enough information, with an evidence-grounded explanation and review flags. The performance panel reports a separate evaluation on 20 labeled sample cases.

Powered by Gemini 2.5 vision + a deterministic guardrail layer · images are the source of truth, history only adds risk context.
